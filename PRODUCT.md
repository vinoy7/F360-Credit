# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two sides of one credit relationship, both on desktop (1250px+) during the working day in Seychelles.

- **Wholesalers (Fuse360):** owners and staff of distribution businesses. They invite the retailers and hospitality businesses they supply, track who has shared verified KYB/KYC details, and later offer trade credit within a company-wide policy that limits every user level.
- **Receivers (FusePay):** owners of retailers, cafés, hotels and restaurants. A new business gets an email invite, completes KYB/KYC (online or on paper), is verified by FusePay, then sets up an account. Existing FusePay users get requests in-app. Both decide which suppliers can see their verified details.

## Product Purpose

Fuse360 is the platform; FusePay verifies every business. The Credit area lets a big supplier extend credit to small buyers with confidence: verified identity first, explicit consent to share, then credit inside clear rules. Success: invites get completed, receivers understand exactly what they share and with whom, and nobody shares data by accident.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, calm, efficient. Quiet confidence around money and identity data. Plain, direct language. Nothing flashy; fast to scan; every state obvious.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI/SaaS templates: purple gradients, glows, identical card grids, emoji labels.
- Old-school bank portals: dense grey forms, tiny text, cluttered tables.
- Crypto or neon fintech: dark neon, flashy charts, hype.
- Overly playful consumer apps: illustrations everywhere, bouncy motion, casual tone.

## Design Principles

1. **Consent is explicit.** Sharing identity data always takes a deliberate, confirmed action, and the user can see and undo what they shared.
2. **State before decoration.** Every row and screen shows its status plainly (requested, in progress, shared, removed); visuals exist to make state readable.
3. **Fit the Fuse system.** Follow the existing Fuse360 / Fuse Design System layouts, components and type (DM Sans, violet accent) rather than inventing new patterns.
4. **Fast for repeat work.** Wholesalers invite many vendors; tables, search and dialogs favour scanning and keyboard use over ornament.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Text contrast at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text and UI component boundaries), visible focus on every control, full keyboard operation including dialogs and drawers, status never conveyed by colour alone, target size at least 24x24px, and reduced-motion respected.
